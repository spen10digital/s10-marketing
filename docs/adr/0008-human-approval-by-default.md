# ADR 0008: Human approval by default

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Source:** docs/02-technical-architecture.md §15

## Decision
Posts require approval before publishing. Auto-publish is only possible through explicit, capped automation policies that need confirmation to enable.

## Why
Brand safety while AI drafts content; Meta policy risk.

## Consequences
Slower throughput until owners trust specific automation rules.
